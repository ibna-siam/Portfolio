### Case Study 1: Financial Statement Analysis — Square Pharmaceuticals Ltd.
- **Project Title:** 3-Year Comprehensive Financial Statement Analysis: Square Pharmaceuticals Ltd.
- **Project Type & Status:** Academic Research Case Study (Academic Secondary Analysis)
- **Business / Academic Context:** Public company analysis using published annual reports of Square Pharmaceuticals Ltd. listed on the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) for fiscal years 2021, 2022, and 2023.
- **Problem Statement:** Evaluating profitability, liquidity, solvency, and operational efficiency trends in Bangladesh's pharmaceutical sector following macroeconomic recovery.
- **Data Sources:** Public Annual Reports (FY2021–2023) published by Square Pharmaceuticals Ltd. via the DSE disclosure portal.
- **Assumptions & Limitations:** Secondary analysis based entirely on consolidated public disclosures; internal management cost-accounting records were not accessible.
- **Ratios in Source Data:**
  - Gross Profit Margin: 38.2% (2021), 39.7% (2022), 41.3% (2023)
  - Net Profit Margin: 12.4% (2021), 14.1% (2022), 15.8% (2023)
  - Return on Equity (ROE): 18.2% (2021), 20.5% (2022), 22.7% (2023)
  - Return on Assets (ROA): 11.3% (2021), 13.0% (2022), 14.6% (2023)
  - Current Ratio: 1.89x (2021), 2.02x (2022), 2.10x (2023)
  - Debt-to-Equity: 0.41x (2021), 0.38x (2022), 0.34x (2023)
  - Inventory Turnover: 6.8x (2021), 6.5x (2022), 6.1x (2023)
- **Validation Finding:** Ratios are documented in source code, but the raw Excel workbook and specific page citations from Square Pharma annual reports are not in the repository.
- **Integrity Correction:** The narrative claim in source code (`"Identified 3 key cost reduction opportunities saving an estimated 8% overhead"`) is an unsubstantiated student boilerplate outcome. A student project cannot claim to have reduced overhead for an independent public corporation.  
  *Marked as:* `[FINANCIAL FIGURE — VALIDATION REQUIRED: UNVERIFIED CORPORATE SAVINGS CLAIM EXCLUDED]`.
- **Evidence Required:** Excel workbook containing formula calculations and citations to official DSE/Square Pharma annual reports.

### Case Study 4: RMG Factory Cost Accounting & CVP Study
- **Project Title:** Unit Cost Sheet & CVP Product Mix Optimization: StyleTex Ltd.
- **Project Type & Status:** Simulated Industrial Costing Study
- **Context:** Simulated cost structure for 'StyleTex Ltd.', a hypothetical garment manufacturer in Gazipur producing shirts, trousers, and jackets.
- **Validation Finding — CRITICAL CVP FORMULA DISCREPANCY IN SOURCE DATA:**
  - In standard managerial accounting, Contribution Margin = Selling Price - Variable Cost (Direct Material + Direct Labor + Variable Overhead).
  - For Shirt: SP (1,500) - VC (680 + 320 + 120 = 1,120) = 380. But source code table row 305 states Contribution Margin is `"420 (28%)"`.
  - For Trouser: SP (2,200) - VC (920 + 480 + 160 = 1,560) = 640. Source code table states `"580 (26.4%)"`.
  - For Jacket: SP (3,400) - VC (1,450 + 680 + 200 = 2,330) = 1,070. Source code table states `"850 (25%)"`.
  - Furthermore, outcome claim (`"Recommended product mix change projected to increase contribution by 18%"`) lacks mathematical demonstration in the repository.
- **Integrity Correction:** Marked as `[FINANCIAL FIGURE — VALIDATION REQUIRED: CVP FORMULA DISCREPANCY]`. Reconcile variable cost deductions and absorption formulas.
- **Evidence Required:** Unit cost sheet and CVP break-even workbook demonstrating absorption rates and product mix optimization.
